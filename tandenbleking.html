About the Treatment

Why choose professional whitening?

Teeth whitening at Global Smile Care goes beyond aesthetics. We use a closed system that protects tooth enamel while targeting stubborn stains deep within the structure.

Drawing on years of experience in aesthetic dentistry, we deliver a result that is both natural and lasting, tailored to the unique shade of your teeth.

Frequently
Asked Questions

Our treatment is designed to be as comfortable as possible. Most patients experience no pain. Patients with sensitive teeth receive a desensitising treatment beforehand.

A full in-office whitening session takes an average of 60 to 90 minutes. You leave our studio with a visible result on the same day.

With proper care and adherence to our aftercare advice, results typically last 12 to 24 months. An annual touch-up session can extend the results.

During the consultation we assess whether you are a suitable candidate. Crowns, bridges and composite fillings are not whitened. We advise pregnant women to postpone the treatment.
